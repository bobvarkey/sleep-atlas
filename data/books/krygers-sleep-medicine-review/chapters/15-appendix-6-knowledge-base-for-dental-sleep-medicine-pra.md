## Practitioners

This is a listing of the examination content areas of American Board of Dental Sleep Medicine (ABDSM) Certification Examination. Information about the exam can be obtained at http://www.abdsm.org/ExamProcess.aspx.

The latest ABDSM guidelines can be found at http://www.abdsm.org/Resources/PDF/ABDSMGuidelines.pdf .

The Reading List recommended by the ABDSM can be found at http://www.abdsm.org/resources/pdf/readinglistfinal.pdf.

The practitioner of dental sleep medicine is expected to be able to do the following (obtained from ABDSM guidelines):

## Skills Tested

1. Analyze polysomnogram reports to understand the patient's diagnosis in order to effectively manage sleep-disordered breathing.

2. Complete and interpret a thorough dental sleep medicine history,

examination, and diagnostic test in order to determine the patient's candidacy for therapeutic intervention.

3. Demonstrate knowledge of published research on treatment alternatives for sleep-disordered breathing.

4. Select oral appliances based on their purpose, their physical features, and their function, and apply proper fitting techniques.

5. Explain the diagnosis and prescribed treatment plan to the patient, and explain indications, contraindications, possible complications, and side effects associated with appliance use.

6. Assess effectiveness, manage potential side effects, and provide long-term followup care.

7. Recognize the appropriate interpretation and role of portable monitoring devices in dental sleep medicine.

8. Understand available approaches to optimizing oral appliance effectiveness.

9. Understand breathing disorders of sleep in children and adolescents, as well as the diagnostic and treatment options for managing these patients.

## Examination Content Areas

The ABDSM certification examination presents 200 multiple choice or true/false questions. The list is modified from http://www.abdsm.org/ExamProcess.aspx. The reader is encouraged to check this website since it is subject to change.

Although this Sleep Medicine Review has content areas that will help in several important parts of the examination, it does not cover all areas of dental sleep medicine.

Recommended resources: Chapter in blue refers to this Review book. ATLAS2 refers to Kryger M, Avidan A, Berry R: Atlas of Clinical Sleep Medicine, ed 2, Philadelphia 2014, Saunders.
