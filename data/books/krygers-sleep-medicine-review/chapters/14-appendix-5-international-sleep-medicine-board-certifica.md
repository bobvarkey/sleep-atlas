The World Sleep Federation (WSF) (http://worldsleepfederation.org), which is made up several sleep societies, has run several exams in different countries over the past few years. Their exam is designed for countries that currently do not have their own sleep medicine exam. The exam is web-based and consists of 100 questions covering the main basic and clinical topic areas relevant to sleep specialists (see Table 1). The table also includes review materials recommended by the authors.
