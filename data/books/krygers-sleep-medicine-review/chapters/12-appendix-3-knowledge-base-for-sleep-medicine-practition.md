The Board and the Sleep Medicine Committee of the European Sleep Research Society (ESRS) have compiled the catalog of knowledge that is expected in sleep medicine clinicians in the European Community. This catalog is cross referenced with educational sources and is modified from Penzel T, Pevernagie D, Dogas Z, et al: Sleep Medicine Committee and The European Sleep Research Society. Catalogue of knowledge and skills for sleep medicine. J Sleep Res 23(2):222-238, 2014. Since the publication of this document, the International Classification of Sleep Disorders, third edition (ICSD3), has been published, and thus some classification terms have been updated.

The authors have added some comments in blue to this document. Chapter in blue refers to this Review book. ATLAS2 refers to Kryger M, Avidan A, Berry R: Atlas of clinical sleep medicine, ed 2, Philadelphia, 2014, Saunders.
